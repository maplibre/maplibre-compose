The camera defines the visible part of the map: a target position, a zoom
level, a bearing, and a tilt. <Api of="rememberMapState" /> creates a
<Api of="MapState" /> that holds the camera. Pass an initial
<Api of="CameraPosition" /> to set the camera position at startup:

<Code code={region(camera, "first-position")} lang="kotlin" title="App.kt" />

## Move the camera

A camera move is instant or animated. <Api of="MapState.setCamera" /> moves the
camera in one step, and <Api of="MapState.animateCamera" /> moves it over a
duration. Both are suspend functions, so call them from a coroutine:

<Code code={region(camera, "animate")} lang="kotlin" title="App.kt" />

## Fit a bounding box

An `animateCamera` overload accepts a `BoundingBox` and animates the camera to
the position that fits it. Padding adds space between the box and the edges of
the map:

<Code code={region(camera, "fit-bounds")} lang="kotlin" title="App.kt" />

A `setCamera` overload fits the same bounding box without an animation.

## Read the visible area

<Api of="MapState.viewport" /> reports the current state of the rendered
map: the size of the map composable, the visible bounding box, and the visible
region. It is null until the map renders its first frame. A composition that
reads it recomposes when the camera moves or the map resizes:

<Code code={region(camera, "viewport")} lang="kotlin" title="App.kt" />
