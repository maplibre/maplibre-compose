title: Draw and measure a shape

The **Feature editing** demo lets you draw a line or polygon, drag its vertices,
and measure its length or area with [Spatial K](https://maplibre.org/spatial-k/).
The presets include Golden Gate Park, JFK Drive, and a circle generated around
the Conservatory of Flowers.

Choose **Polygon** or **Line**, tap the map to place vertices, and choose **Done**.
Drag a vertex to reshape it, or tap one to insert or remove a point. **Simplify**
previews a reduced vertex count against the original outline; **Apply** accepts
it and **Cancel** discards it. **Rotate 15°** uses Spatial K's distance, bearing,
and offset functions. Undo and redo operate on completed edits.

The demo edits one line or single-ring polygon at a time. Its editing state,
vertex input, and geometry operations live together in the demo app so you can
follow how map interactions become edits to a GeoJSON shape.

## Read the example

The source is in
[`demos/featureediting`](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-compose/tree/main/demo-app/common/src/commonMain/kotlin/org/maplibre/compose/demoapp/demos/featureediting):

- `FeatureEditingDemo.kt` draws GeoJSON layers and receives map taps.
- `VertexInput.kt` compares a press with projected vertices and handles their
  drags in screen coordinates. Other gestures remain map gestures.
- `FeatureEditingState.kt` separates the committed shape from drawing and edit
  previews. Accepting a preview adds one undo step; cancellation leaves the
  committed shape unchanged.
- `Shape.kt` converts the open vertex list to GeoJSON, calls Spatial K, and checks
  polygon outlines in the Mercator plane. Invalid previews cannot be committed;
  an invalid drag returns to its starting shape.
- `FeatureEditingPanel.kt` presents measurements and editing controls.
- `Presets.kt` supplies the example shapes.

Applications can adapt these pieces to their own document and save workflow.
See [Handle gestures and clicks](/maplibre-compose/interaction/) for map input
and [Overlay Compose UI](/maplibre-compose/controls/) for overlay placement.
