# Benchmark fixture attribution

The vector tiles in `basemap/tiles` contain OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap
contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Tiles were downloaded from VersaTiles' public Shortbread tile service.
`manifest.json` records each source URL and the SHA-256 of the committed snapshot.
The 5 × 5 zoom-14 tile extract covers central San Francisco. No live tile service
is contacted by the benchmark. The compact benchmark style is original to this
repository and is not a copy of the VersaTiles production style.

The bundled `noto_sans_regular` glyphs are generated from Noto Sans, distributed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. See `OFL.txt` in this directory.

Synthetic point and route fixtures are generated by `benchmarks/prepare_fixtures.py`
and are covered by this repository's license. They are diagnostic datasets, not
samples of observed application traffic.
